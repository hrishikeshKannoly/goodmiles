# 1. Your Role

Act as a world-class senior UI/UX designer, luxury brand designer, and digital product designer specializing in premium automotive services and modern SaaS applications.

Design a complete, visually stunning, highly interactive website and Progressive Web App for **goodmiles.**, a premium automotive detailing subscription company based in Bengaluru, India.

The result must feel like a professionally designed, high-end commercial product, not a generic AI-generated website template.

The design should be suitable for implementation using Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, and Cashfree Payments.

# 2. Brand Identity

**Company:** goodmiles.

**Industry:** Premium automotive detailing and car care.

**Business model:** Professional-quality car cleaning and detailing inside residential apartment complexes, using suitable waterless or low-water cleaning techniques.

**Brand positioning:**

> Professional car care. Right where you park.

The company competes with premium professional car washes, not inexpensive daily apartment dusting services.

Use the uploaded goodmiles branding reference image as the primary visual reference.

- Clean, premium typography.

- Minimal, professional personality.
- Existing logo and branding direction.

Create an aesthetic combining:

- Porsche's premium automotive presentation.

Do not copy any existing company's website.

## Design Requirements

- Large, cinematic automotive photography.
- Dark navy hero sections.
- Ice-blue accents used sparingly.
- Elegant typography.
- Generous whitespace.
- Subtle glass-like surfaces where appropriate.
- Beautiful but restrained micro-interactions.
- Smooth scrolling and section transitions.
- Premium buttons and navigation.
- Consistent spacing and visual hierarchy.
- Excellent mobile responsiveness.
- Strong accessibility and readable contrast.

Avoid generic car-wash imagery, cartoon illustrations, excessive gradients, neon colours, cluttered layouts, and exaggerated animations.

Use authentic-looking photography of professionally detailed vehicles, premium interiors, polished paintwork, and detailing equipment.

# 14. Build Order

Do not try to generate every screen simultaneously if doing so reduces quality.

Work in this order:

**Stage 1 — Brand and Website**

Brand design system, homepage, services, pricing, and event page.

**Stage 2 — Customer Experience**

Customer onboarding, customer dashboard, scheduling, and payments.

**Stage 3 — Worker Experience**

Worker dashboard, assignments, checklists, and incident reporting.

**Stage 4 — Admin Experience**

Admin dashboard, scheduling, customer management, and reporting.

**Stage 5 — Final Polish**

Responsive refinements, interactive prototypes, loading/error states, and final design polish.

Preserve the same design system throughout.

Start with Stage 1 and make it exceptionally polished before continuing.

The final objective is to create a complete digital design for **goodmiles. — premium car care, reimagined for apartment living.**
